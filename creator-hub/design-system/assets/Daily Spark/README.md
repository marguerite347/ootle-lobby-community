## Legacy shipped SVG plates

Reward art for the Daily Spark vault spinner, v4 (authored SVG, no stock; generated by `creator-hub/hub/scripts/daily-spark-art/generate.py`). Use only inside that feature. Fixed inks in `tari-ink`, `tari-cloud`, `tari-purple` and `tari-green` with a cool silver/lavender foil — never muddy gold. Labels are outlined Poppins 800, so they render the same in any `<img>`.

- `vault-disc-face.svg` — spinner face, wedges clockwise from the top `1×, 2×, 1×, 3×, 2×, 5×`, matching the server's multipliers. Tiers read by shape: 1× plain, 2× one pip, 3× two lime pips, 5× a lime sunburst wedge with an ink label. Labels are radial, so the landed wedge reads upright under the pointer. Never change a label without changing the real odds. Shown at 82% of the disc.
- `vault-disc-rim.svg` — foil rim with lit marquee bulbs, notches on the spoke lines and the fused lime **5× · SUPER PATH** plaque centred on the 5× wedge (330°). Spins with the face at 100% of the disc.
- `vault-pointer-lime.svg` — lime blade under a foil cap, pointing down into the disc; 28×24px at the top edge.
- `vault-gateway-badge.svg` — cutout-ticket "5× · SUPER PATH" badge, the fallback when the rim image fails.
- `vault-charge-hero.svg` — the Vault Charge crystal: lit facets, lime energy core, orbit ring and foil plinth. Shown whole in the reactor, and cropped to its core in the disc hub at 34% of the disc.
