# RemixInvitationCard

Portal-style link card inviting people to explore originals and their Riffs, over an isometric "original vs Riff" illustration.

Static rendition of `components/RemixInvitation.tsx` (the optional preview video is omitted; without it the card uses its gradient). No props.

- Radius 28px on a deep purple radial; eyebrow "A spark. A Riff." then a plain line saying where it goes.
- Two floating isometric worlds, lilac for the original and green for the Riff, joined by a dotted thread. The lime arrow disc rotates slightly on hover.
- All float, flow and hover motion stops under reduced motion and effects off, and the video pauses with effects off.
