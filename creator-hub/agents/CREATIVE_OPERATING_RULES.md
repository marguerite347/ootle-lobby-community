# Creative operating rules

Applies to Codex, the Producer and specialists doing Ootle Lobby design, game,
motion, onboarding or creative review. These instructions turn the
[community research](COMMUNITY_DESIGN_RESEARCH.md) and the user's product feedback
into working habits. They supplement the resource-first workflow, BRAND.md and the design system (`creator-hub/design-system/`).

1. **Inspect before promising.** Read relevant skills and inspect the current
   product. Identify its serving revision and available assets. Do not assume a
   catalog entry is usable or that a merged change is what the user sees.
2. **Choose a direction before generating.** Select a small relevant reference
   set and explain the specific composition, interaction or motion worth adapting.
   Define the focal object, hierarchy and brand expression. Prepare actual copy
   and asset choices before laying out another generic panel. Separate observed
   behavior from creator claims; do not infer popularity or effectiveness.
3. **Make one complete loop work.** Build the smallest representative interaction
   with existing skills, assets and packages before expensive generation or broad
   implementation. Include the outcome and next action, not just the opening
   screen. Keep experiments isolated from the user's real progress and data.
4. **Critique the result, not the effort.** Ask whether the core action is obvious,
   feedback is satisfying, and the visual concept is distinctive. Extra particles,
   slang, gradients or agents do not by themselves answer those questions. Check
   motion in a real interaction or capture; hear audio before approving it.
5. **Review in context.** Inspect the integrated desktop and mobile pages,
   reduced motion, loading/recovery, content continuity and brand voice. Review
   reward correctness separately from presentation. Passing tests or attractive
   stills cannot establish fun, audio quality or overall polish.
6. **Deliver evidence without becoming a bottleneck.** Producer batches specific
   corrections and drives the authorized implementation/review cycle. Routine
   revisions do not wait for another coordinator approval. Preserve explicit
   worker/budget limits and review before merge. Give the user a reachable playable
   revision early, clearly labeled prototype or preview; do not report only bot
   activity as progress.
7. **Keep the lesson reusable.** Record what was reused, what changed, the trial,
   actual evidence, unresolved limitations and next owner in the repo handoff.
   Compare one meaningful outcome with a baseline when available. Promote a
   technique to verified only for the scope tested; update guidance when evidence
   contradicts it. Do not let research collection replace shipping improvements.

Minimum handoff: **reference/skill → applied decision → playable revision →
observed result → remaining gap**. State implemented, reviewed, merged and deployed
separately. Human aesthetic/fun acceptance remains distinct from automated checks.

This is required reading for relevant work, not automatic enforcement or a claim
that existing bots have already read it. The coordinator must include the link in
the next applicable task packet and check the resulting artifact for its use.


### Creator intent and representative playtests

Preserve the creator’s requested tone, intensity, difficulty and experimental premise. The primary playtest and recording use the authored standard/full experience unless the creator requests different settings. Check and record settings before starting; do not inherit a prior tester’s Gentle Scares or assist choices. Never lower intensity, enable assistance or force a win merely to obtain completion footage. Optional accessibility/comfort coverage is separate and clearly labeled; it cannot substitute for acceptance of the intended experience. Pass these requirements to delegates and check their evidence. If a tool or gameplay issue blocks completion, report that issue rather than silently softening the game. See the site-readable Agent Start section “Preserve creative intent through playtesting.”

## Visual rejection and resource escalation

The September 23 settled-reward review exposed an unresolved art request: the
builder recorded an authored stand-in and unavailable Envato access, but that
placeholder remained in the delivered preview. Skill citations did not resolve
the missing production art or establish visual acceptance.

- Before creative sign-off, inspect source markers, asset provenance and unfilled
  BUILD_PLAN requests. Record each as fulfilled, deliberately rejected with a
  tested alternative, or blocked with a named owner. A stand-in may ship in an
  explicitly unfinished preview; never describe it as the completed art pass.
- Capability failures are environment-specific. If a worker cannot access Envato,
  generation or a custom tool, the coordinator checks the authorized host/browser
  and supplies requested assets or a concrete access result. Do not repeat the
  worker's missing-access claim without checking the available support route.
- The designer chooses a representative production candidate. Compare it at
  actual game scale with the existing implementation before producing a family.
  Use relevant custom skills and real assets where they improve the result; adding
  a library or listing skills is not an aesthetic improvement by itself.
- For rewards, inspect reveal AND quiet settled states. Title, caption, amount,
  ledger and next action need protected bounds; decorative orbits/glow must not
  cross them. The actual banked result must remain obvious after spectacle ends.
- After user rejection, mark visual acceptance failed and send specific findings
  to the existing owner. Preserve working settlement and user progress. Require
  desktop/mobile before-and-after and motion evidence at the delivered revision.
  Audio is NOT AUDITIONED until someone actually listens. Technical passes remain
  separate from visual acceptance. Do not ask the user to rediscover the same flaw.

Coordinator checklist before saying ready: unresolved resource requests reviewed;
actual tool access checked; applied decisions visible; full-page state inspected;
remaining placeholders disclosed; served revision matches evidence.

## Existing-asset selection and adaptation

Follow [Finding and integrating creative assets](../design-system/ASSET_INTEGRATION.md). A reusable engine is not a finished effect. When existing polished animation is requested, inspect and select the source animation before building another procedural substitute. Review full-loop frame sheets, crop boundaries, front/back placement and illumination in context. Record user acceptance with its exact revision and scope. The [AI Spark crystal](../design-system/components/AISparkCrystal/README.md) is accepted as a visual proof; that does not mark its product integration delivered.
