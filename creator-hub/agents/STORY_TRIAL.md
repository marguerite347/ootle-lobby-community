## Revised audition (Grok-authored, observed in app)

### Comic heist

You’re a glitter-gloved thief with one Hot Potato Charge. Bank it in the getaway van and the score stays yours—boring, solvent, alive. Risk it: slap the charge on the vault lock. Win and the door yawns like a casino smile. Fail and the charge cooks itself into useless sparks while the alarm hiccups a ridiculous kazoo fanfare. Restart? Fresh gloves, empty pockets, same vault humming. The rules never change. Only your nerve does.

### Eerie science fiction

In the under-lab, you carry a single coherent charge—warm as a pulse. Dock it in the safe cradle and the run continues: quiet, incomplete, intact. Feed it to the vault aperture and reality softens; success opens the sealed chamber of unfinished selves. Fail, and the charge decoheres into cold static; the sealed door stays blank. Restart empties the cradle—full reset, no memory kept. Any lingering dread is flavor only, not persistent state.
