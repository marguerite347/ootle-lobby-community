# Market research and promotion

## Revenue benchmarking

[The Steam Revenue Report](https://weloveit.io/developers/steam-revenue-report/) by Ichiro Lambe, Matt Martindale and Tim Ermilov. Page read 2026-09-19.

Use for genre comparisons, market concentration and scenario research. Its revenue figures are estimates derived from reviews and adjustable multipliers, not audited earnings or developer profit. The source discusses missing zero-review games and unequal observation time for recent releases. Record methodology, multiplier, cohort and snapshot date when citing a number; do not import these estimates as Tari revenue actuals or promised game outcomes. Not affiliated with Valve.
